Inventory for eBay Privacy Policy

Inventory uses authorized eBay seller data to manage listings and measure listing experiments.

Data and storage

The hosted runner stores seller authorization and uses listing information and selling analytics to support approved operations. Seller authorization is encrypted in storage.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke Inventory access in your eBay account settings. The runner processes verified eBay account deletion notifications to remove matching account data held by the runner.
